<objective>
Wire the authority layer into the CLI entry point and the Claude dispatch path, and fix the
timeout arithmetic that would otherwise make the watchdog unobservable.

Purpose: without this plan every piece built in plans 02-01 and 02-04 through 02-07 exists
but is never constructed in production. It also closes RESEARCH B3: today
`_TOOL_TIMEOUTS["set_aircraft_control"]` is 5.0, identical to `send_command`'s own
`asyncio.wait_for` deadline, and the outer one starts first — so on a genuine ack timeout
the tool layer cancels the dispatch, `send_command`'s `except TimeoutError` never runs, and
the watchdog counter never increments. Worse, a *successful* command with a verification
rule polls for up to the verifier timeout on top of the ack round trip, so a slow success
can also blow a 5-second tool budget.

Output: `ClaudeClient` accepting and forwarding an `AuthorityState`, config-driven timeouts
with a structural guard on their ordering, CLI construction and subscription, and the
CLAUDE.md updates this phase owes.
</objective>
