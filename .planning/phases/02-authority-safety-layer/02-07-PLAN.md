<objective>
Close the second write path. `ProcedureExecutor._execute_step` currently calls
`_resolve_command` and `send_command` directly, reaching SimConnect with no safety check,
no verification, no history and — without this plan — no authority gate.

Purpose: D-04 and D-06. This is not a hypothetical: `command_safety.py` was wired into
`tools.py` in v1.3 Phase 1 and never into `procedures.py`, and nothing detected it for
months. Routing through `set_aircraft_control` closes it, and a structural guard keeps it
closed. D-06 then overrides the executor's documented continue-on-failure default for the
withheld case specifically: a failed step means the sim did not take it, while a withheld
step means MERLIN has decided it should not be acting unsupervised — and continuing past
that is precisely acting unsupervised.

Output: a collaborator-injected `ProcedureExecutor`, a third step outcome distinct from
failure, an abort-on-withheld loop, and the tests that pin all three.
</objective>
