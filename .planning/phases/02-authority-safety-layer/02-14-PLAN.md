<objective>
Make authority bounded for the commands this phase itself made reachable.

Purpose: the phase goal says MERLIN's authority must be "explicit, **bounded**, and never
ambiguous". Two things in the shipped code contradict the middle word.

`parking_brake` resolves every action — `"on"`, `"off"`, `"release"`, `"toggle"` — to the same
`PARKING_BRAKES` toggle event. It is in `CRITICAL_COMMANDS`, in the `set_aircraft_control` enum,
and registered in the adapter's `CommandMap`. It is absent from `UNCONFIRMABLE_POSITION_SYSTEMS`
and absent from `DEFAULT_RULES`. This is bit-for-bit the CMD-08 defect that `carb_heat` and
`fuel_pump` were refused for — except those two are unreachable (not in the enum, not
registered) while this one is live. "Parking brake off" on landing rollout sets the brake.

Plan 02-02's CMD-07 work registered `FUEL_SELECTOR_OFF`, `FUEL_SELECTOR_ALL/LEFT/RIGHT/SET` and
`CROSS_FEED_OPEN/OFF/TOGGLE` in the adapter. Before that change they NACKed; now
`TransmitClientEvent` fires for real, and `fuel_selector` and `crossfeed` are already in the tool
enum. Meanwhile `MAGNETO_SET` was deliberately withheld with the stated reason that registering
it "turns a named tool call into a working in-flight engine shutdown with nothing in front of
it". `fuel_selector off` in flight is an in-flight engine shutdown by another route, and it has
*less* in front of it than magnetos would: `DEFAULT_RULES` has no entry for it, the default
`AUTHORITY_LEVEL` is `full`, and `assisted` cannot withhold what no rule flags. The
reachable/deferred split has to follow one severity rationale; today it follows two.

Output: `parking_brake` restricted to an explicit toggle with the same actionable refusal
`carb_heat` gets, six new safety rules covering the fuel, mixture and parking-brake surface, and
the four documentation tables that currently describe the old behaviour brought back into line.
</objective>
